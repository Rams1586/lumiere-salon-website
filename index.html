The studio

A chair, a mirror,
and no rush.

Scissors opened in 2017 on a simple idea: a neighbourhood salon should feel like your own bathroom mirror, only better lit, and staffed by people who actually know what they're doing. We keep the appointment book small on purpose — six chairs, never overbooked — so every service gets the time it needs.

Trained hands

Every stylist completes ongoing certification in colour theory and modern cutting technique.

Honest products

We use and sell only sulphate-free, dermat-approved lines — no filler brands, no upselling.

Real scheduling

Book online, get a WhatsApp reminder, and walk in without waiting past your slot.
